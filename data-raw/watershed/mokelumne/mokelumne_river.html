Instream Spawning and Rearing Habitat

Data Source: LMR_HSI_2015conditions.xlsx

Data was provided by East Bay Municipal Utility District for Fall Run HSI-Flow relationships for Chinook Salmon. The data represents pre-2018 early implementation conditions. The suitability criteria (Table 1) used is the same as that applied for the Health Rivers and Landscapes program Mokelumne River assessment.

Table 1. Habitat suitability indices (HSI) applied to spawning, in channel rearing, and floodplain rearing to get the total suitable habitat area relationships
